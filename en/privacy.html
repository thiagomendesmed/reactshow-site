YOUR DATA

Privacy Policy

ReactShow does not collect any user data.

Last updated October 2, 2026.

On-device processing

ReactShow uses the camera, microphone, photos and videos to create and save your reaction videos. This processing happens on your device. The app does not send your recordings to the developer's servers.

Permissions

  • Camera: to capture your reaction.
  • Microphone: to record your reaction's audio.
  • Photos and files: to select the content used in the video.
  • Save to Photos: to add the final video to your library.

You can manage permissions in the iPhone Settings app. Turning a permission off may stop the related feature from working.

ReactShow Pro subscription

Purchases and renewals are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. The developer does not receive your payment details. The app checks with Apple, from your device, only whether your subscription is active in order to unlock Pro features. This information is handled under Apple's privacy policy.

Videos and preferences

App preferences, teleprompter scripts and drafts are stored locally. Videos saved to Photos follow the storage and sync settings you use on your device, including any services you have chosen to turn on.

When you share a video with another app, that app handles the content under its own privacy practices.

This website and support

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical access information under its privacy statement. If you open a support request on GitHub, your profile and what you send will be publicly visible. Don't share sensitive information there.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, use the ReactShow support channel, maintained by the developer, Thiago Mendes.

If the app's practices change, this policy and the privacy information on the App Store will be updated.

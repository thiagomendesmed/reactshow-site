Record your reaction on video, from framing to the final file.

Start recording

  1. Allow camera and microphone access when ReactShow asks.
  2. Pick a video or photo from Photos or Files, or use Camera only mode.
  3. Adjust the layout, choose the aspect ratio and tap Record.
  4. Stop recording, review the result and save it to Photos.

Frequently asked questions

The camera or microphone doesn't work

Check ReactShow's permissions in the iPhone Settings app. If another app is recording, stop it before trying again.

I can't save the video

Check the permission to add photos and videos and the free space on your device. Keep the app open until the export finishes.

I can't find dual-camera recording

Recording with both cameras at once depends on your iPhone model. All other modes work with a single camera.

How do I cancel ReactShow Pro?

The subscription is managed by Apple. Open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › ReactShow and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid being charged for the next period.

I subscribed but Pro features don't show up

In the app's Settings, tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account used to buy. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Which iPhone can I use?

ReactShow requires iOS 17 or later. Some features depend on the device.

Which languages does the app support?

English, Spanish and Portuguese. The app follows your iPhone's language; in any other language it appears in English.

Need help?

Send your question through the support channel on GitHub. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and the steps to reproduce the problem.
